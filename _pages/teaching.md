### Teaching

I have served as a teaching assistant for several courses during my time at UNH. These include 
Data Science with R in Life Sciences (BIOL 706/806), Anatomy and Physiology (BMS 507/508), and 
Applied Biostatistics I (BIOL 528). These were both undergraduate and graduate courses. Tasks for these courses
included leading programming demonstrations and lab activities, grading, teaching lessons to complement those
taught in lecture, and creating exercises for student retention.

### Mentoring

I have mentored two undergradute students one-on-one. Caylin Grove (2022-2025, UNH BS Genomics) studied genomic tests 
for MSX & Dermo in eastern oyster larvae and is a current masters student at University of Pittsburgh. Lauren Welch
(2021-2022, UNH BS Genetics) studied the isolation of eastern oyster larvae for downstream genomic testing. She then 
earned her masters in Forensic Science at University of New Haven. In addition, I have provided one-on-one instruction
to multiple graduate students to aid in the genomics aspect of their research projects.

### Outreach

My most significant teaching experience was a 2022 outreach program I designed 
(titled Day as a Scientist, [press release here](https://issuu.com/sschaier/docs/unh-colsa_thrive-spr24_web/10?e=12&o=1)) 
and led for students from a nearby minority-serving high school. 
After preparing them with relevant biology lessons, I brought them to the Jackson Marine 
Laboratory at the University of New Hampshire for a day of hands-on research, which included 
counting and sexing green crabs for a citizen science project, building 3D ocean floor maps, 
and designing microbes out of household supplies based on survival needs. Student feedback was 
strongly positive, and several students specifically noted how meaningful it was to see scientists 
who “looked like them” at work.

In addition, I have served as an invited Guest Judge for the UNH undergraduate research conference (2023, 2024), 
a judge for the Junior Science & Humanities Symposium (2022), was a member of the US Aquaculture Society Student 
Activities Committee (2022-2024), and was the UNH Ecological Genomics Reading Group Leader (2022-2024).
