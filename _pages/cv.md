## Research Interests

**Population and conservation genomics under environmental change**\
Utilizing genomic and transcriptomic tools to characterize population structure, adaptive capacity, and disease dynamics in marine organisms\
Identifying the standing genetic variation and epigenetic mechanisms that shape resilience to climate-driven and anthropogenic stressors\
**Quantitative and theoretical marine ecology**\
Integrating differential gene expression, metagenomic, and environmental time-series data with theoretical and statistical modeling\
Using different approaches to understand how disturbance regimes, climate variability, and pathogen pressure interact to structure marine populations and ecosystems\
**Open, computational approaches to genomic discovery**\
Developing and applying bioinformatic pipelines and machine learning/neural network approaches\
Prioritizing and contributing to open-access, reproducible programming practices
